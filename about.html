NextWatch shows the well-rated movies and shows already included with your streaming services, so you can stop flipping between apps and pick something in a minute.

Find something to watch

How it works

1

Tell it your services

Netflix, Max, Prime and the rest. It only lists titles included with your subscriptions, never ones to rent or buy.

2

It filters out the duds

Every title is checked against IMDb and Rotten Tomatoes, so you only see what scores well.

3

Pick and mark

Mark titles Seen, Want or Not interested. Your lists keep up as you watch.

What's inside

🌙

Tonight's pick

One suggestion a day, chosen from your want list first, plus rows like “Because you watched…”, new releases and hidden gems.

👫

Watch with your partner

Link accounts to see what you both want, what one of you has already seen, and a Watch together list of things neither of you has seen.

🎬

Sections and moods

Movies, series, anime, docs, true crime, reality and kids. Filter by mood, length, genre, director or year.

🆕

New seasons

When a show you've watched gets a new season, it comes back to your list, along with what's coming soon.

▶️

A YouTube page

Trending and most-viewed videos, plus History, Food & travel, Soccer and F1, with the latest match and race highlights.

↻

Always current

The catalog, scores and services refresh every night, or right away when you tap Sync.

📱 Put it on your home screen

iPhone or iPad: open NextWatch in Safari, tap the Share button, then “Add to Home Screen”.

Android: in Chrome, tap ⋮, then “Install app” (or “Add to Home screen”).

The first time you open it from your home screen, sign in with your email and password.
