Why PopcornDate

The popcorn's ready, you're both on the couch, and nobody can decide. You look up what's highly rated, find it on a streaming service, and then see it's only available to rent or buy. Or one of you has already seen it.

PopcornDate shows only the good stuff that's already included with the services you pay for. Link up with your partner and it keeps track of what you each want and what you've already watched, so picking something together takes a minute, not the whole evening.

How it works

1

Tell it your services

Netflix, Max, Prime and the rest. It only lists titles included with your subscriptions, never ones to rent or buy.

2

It filters out the duds

Every title is checked against IMDb and Rotten Tomatoes, so you only see what scores well.

3

Match and watch

You each tap Want on what looks good. Anything you both want lands in Our list, ready for date night.

What's inside

Tonight's pick

One suggestion a day, chosen from your want list first, plus rows like “Because you watched…”, new releases and hidden gems.

Made for two

Link accounts to get Our list (titles you both want), a New to both of you view of things neither of you has seen, and each other's favorites.

Sections and moods

Movies, series, anime, docs, true crime, reality and kids. Filter by mood, length, genre, director or year.

New seasons

When a show you've watched gets a new season, it comes back to your list, along with what's coming soon.

A YouTube page

Trending and most-viewed videos in English, plus History, Food & travel, Soccer, F1, Politics and full episodes from popular US and UK podcasts. Follow channels you love, like SNL or a favorite podcast, to see their newest videos.

Always current

The catalog, scores and services refresh every night, or right away when you tap Sync.

Put it on your home screen

iPhone or iPad: open PopcornDate in Safari, tap the Share button, then “Add to Home Screen”.

Android: in Chrome, tap ⋮, then “Install app” (or “Add to Home screen”).

The first time you open it from your home screen, sign in with your email and password.
